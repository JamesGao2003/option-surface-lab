05 / DATA & METHOD

What this snapshot can establish

Two different prices

MID_PRICE is the closing NBBO midpoint, (bid + ask) / 2, under the assignment’s field definition; it may reflect stale quotes and is not an exchange settlement or an executable fill.

TRDPRC_1 is the RIC’s last recorded daily trade under the assignment’s convention; it need not occur at the close, and daily fields do not identify a morning execution price.

An explicit denominator

The displayed percentage is 100 × (mid present and trade missing) / sampled active series for the selected date and option type.

A sampled active series has at least one observed price on or before the selected date and has not expired, including expiry day; this first-observation-to-expiry interval is an inferred listing window, not a verified historical listing record.

The exact percentage across all listed contracts cannot be recovered from this cache; neither synthetic candidates nor all-null response columns prove a listing, and the paired median excludes missing fields without filling them.

Coverage and reproducibility

Missing values stay missing; zeros stay zeros; there is no forward fill or interpolated price sheet. No live API requests or credentials are needed to view this page.

The cache lacks a corporate-action history, so split-adjusted contract coverage is unverified; synthetic identifiers are limited to the assignment’s stated convention and Friday expiries.
